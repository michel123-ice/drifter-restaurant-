Drifters Kitchen Bar

Family-run restaurant and bar in East Wittering, a stone's throw from the beach.

Book a table See the menus

Contemporary, rustic, industrial.

A contemporary, rustic and industrial setting with a large decking space, made for a sundowner or a catch up close to the beach.

Drifters is run by a family, from the first coffee of the day to the last table outside in the evening.

The decking is the biggest space in the place, and it is made to be used in all weather. When the evening turns cool, there are blankets out there so you can stay a while longer.

The outside is why people come.

A big space, open to the sky, a few minutes from the sand. Lunch in the sun, a drink as the light goes, and a table that stays yours a little longer.
